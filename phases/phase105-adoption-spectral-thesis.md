# Phase 105 (PROPOSED) — the adoption: geometry as data, and the spectral successor program

*Working session, 2026-07-24, continuing the phase-104 session. **Status: PROPOSED; the adoption decision below was taken by the human signatory in-session ("adopt it"); the document remains contingent at one point on the pending JOIN-4a referee pass (exhibit 4 of §2), and nothing here amends the closed record through phase 103.** This phase does what the record has done at every fork: states the thesis it is adopting, tags it, arms the kill against it before any work is invested, and names its weak points in the same breath as its hopes.*

---

## 1. The adopted thesis

> **The data principle [adopted; conjecture-level; kill armed in §5].** For the carrier universe, the constructible world has exactly **one** continuous dynamics — the modular clock — and **every other structure it carries is static data**: invariants read from the clock's own record (spectrum, cohomology, winding), never flows. Space is not a stage that things move through; space is the *content of the record the clock reads*. Matter is not events in space; matter is *decoration on the record* — classes, charges, windings. The successor program studies the carrier as a **spectral universe**: geometry reconstructed from data, physics sought as functionals of data, and "motion" expected — if anywhere — only as an effective, coarse-grained description of reading.

The pivot this represents, stated without softening: the original program bet that geometry *generates* physics dynamically (the join). That bet died at phase 103 for everything constructible. The adopted thesis bets the opposite reading of the same theorems: the theorems were not obstructions but *information* — four independent rungs of machinery each reported the same ontology, and the program was refusing the report.

## 2. The evidence adopted — the four exhibits

1. **Lengths in the clock** (phase 102, refereed): the geodesic length spectrum e^{∓ℓ(γ)} sits in the modular spectrum; the marked length spectrum determines the hyperbolic surface. The clock archives the whole geometry; the flow moves none of it.
2. **The census** (phase 103, refereed, kill honored): no constructible flow moves the base continuously. Space cannot be traversed by anything buildable — only read.
3. **The sector ceiling** (phase 104 §7.2, refereed): local III₁ rigidity — sectors carry no new local dynamics; a sector *is* a gluing-cohomology class. Data by theorem.
4. **The soliton** (phase 104 JOIN-4a, **referee pending — the one contingency**): a particle coupled into the toy arena leaves the base inert; its charge is provably non-localizable away from the fixed points and survives exactly as the winding class of a ℤ-cocycle. The particle decorates the record; it does not move it.

Two readings of the four exhibits were possible: a *ceiling of the tools* (the constructible machinery is blind to dynamics that exist), or a *fact about the object* (there is no dynamics to see). **Adoption is the bet on the second reading.** The bet is falsifiable, and its falsifier is already posed: see §5.

## 3. What adoption reinterprets — the record's shape, explained

Under the data principle, the pattern of what survived the program's failures stops being a coincidence:

- **The DTAB paper** — the record's most credible artifact — is no longer a side product of a failed physics program. It is a page of the *spectrum of the data universe*: the complete d-invariant table of the protected states, per fold count and torsion sector. Under the adopted thesis, computing such tables **is** studying the physics-bearing layer.
- **The statistics census** (bosonic/fermionic counts per flux sector, one fermion pair per handle) — data. **NOTE-1's** modular conjugation — data (an involution class). **The T-2π lock** — data (a forced ratio). **REMARK-1's extremality** — data (a rank). Everything that survived four red-team campaigns is an invariant; everything that died was a dynamical claim. The record sorted itself along exactly the line the thesis draws, before the thesis existed.
- **The knot/defect ontology** aligns rather than competes: defects as decorations with quantized data (charge, chirality, Bennequin budget) was always a data-description; what died was only the demand that the decorated record also *flow*.

## 4. The successor work items

> **SPEC-1 — the reconstruction theorem [the crown target; posed].** Make "space is the content of the record" a theorem: construct a spectral triple on the carrier's algebras whose Connes distance recovers the hyperbolic geometry from constructible data (the modular/length spectrum of phase 102 as input). Honest caveat, logged now: the *unmarked* length spectrum determines the surface only up to finite isospectral ambiguity (Vignéras); the reconstruction statement must either obtain the marking from the algebra (the conjugacy-class labeling of the u_γ is a candidate marking) or state the finite ambiguity as part of the theorem. Adjacent established corpus: noncommutative geometry of foliations and boundary actions (Connes; Connes–Moscovici) — technology exists; the carrier-specific triple does not.
>
> **JOIN-4a′ — the first dictionary entry [posed; computation-sized].** Evaluate the winding phase Θ_t(q) of the JOIN-4a cocycle explicitly: the pairing between charge q and geodesic length ℓ(γ) in the one place both live (the T-2π geometry). If the pairing is forced (no normalization freedom), it is the first entry of the data dictionary — matter data paired with geometry data by theorem. [Contingent on the JOIN-4a referee confirming the cocycle computation; also inherits the coboundary question — if the class trivializes, this entry is empty and says so.]
>
> **SPEC-ACT — the bridge from data to physics [posed; the successor to the join].** The known mechanism by which spectral data yields physical functionals is the **spectral action principle** (Chamseddine–Connes): S = Tr f(D/Λ) — action functionals, including gravitational terms, from nothing but the spectrum of a Dirac-type operator. The successor to the dead join is therefore a *computation with existing technology*: build the carrier's Dirac operator (SPEC-1's triple) and evaluate its spectral action. What the old join was — a hope with no method — this is not: the method exists and has produced Einstein–Hilbert terms in other geometries. Whether it produces anything *right* here is the bet, and it is computable. Note where the one-scale problem re-enters, honestly: the spectral action carries a cutoff Λ — the calibration question (phase-104 session's rods-and-clocks analysis) returns as the physical meaning of Λ, with the forced internal ratios (T-2π, e^{∓ℓ(γ)}) as the only honest inputs.
>
> **DATA-DICT — the matter map, recast [reframed, still far].** The old matter map ("nothing exists") demanded dynamics: knots → propagating particles. The recast demands classification: defect invariants (winding classes, Arf/spin data, DTAB sectors) → observed quantum numbers. Strictly more modest, and now aligned with the only thing sectors can carry (exhibit 3). G-SEL and the far rungs remain exactly as ledgered — adoption does not pay those debts, it re-denominates them.

**The sky is untouched, again.** A1/A2 (Euclid, months), κ = 0 (JUNO), the null commitments — none routed through the join, none route through the thesis. They remain the empirical face and can still kill the framework outright.

JOIN-4b is hereby re-scoped: no longer the escape hatch of the old thesis, but the standing experiment against the new one. (Symmetry note, per house custom: the machinery is again aimed at the current thesis, not at its rivals.)
