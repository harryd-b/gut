## 2. Pairwise compatibility

Verified computations [BOX-2]: Q-lattice spacings are 1 (R=√(2π)), √π (R=√2), √(2π/3) = √6·√π/3 (R=√3). Ratios: √π ≈ 1.7725, √(2π/3) ≈ 1.4472, √(3/2) = √6/2 ≈ 1.2247. The first two are irrational because π is transcendental [others': Lindemann]; the third is irrational because 6 is not a perfect square times a rational square. Hence **any two of the three Q-lattices intersect only at {0}**: if a·m = b·n with a/b irrational and m,n ∈ ℤ, then m = n = 0.

- **1 vs 2** (√(2π) vs √2): not jointly satisfiable by one R. ℤ ∩ √π·ℤ = {0}; equivalently in q-units, (1/√2)ℤ ∩ (1/√(2π))ℤ = {0}. The refereed GAP-N7 note is confirmed in its stated form: **(1/√2)ℤ ∩ ℤ = {0}** (since m/√2 = n forces m = n√2, impossible for √2 irrational unless both vanish) — the bridge lattice misses every nonzero sector of the self-dual arena. Conflict class: **(b) scope-separable** — the abelian bridge record and the SU(2)₁ consistency gate are distinct arenas; nothing refereed forces them onto one lattice. If a single-arena ontology were declared, the conflict becomes (a) genuine.
- **1 vs 3** (√(2π) vs √3): same structure; ℤ ∩ √(2π/3)ℤ = {0}. **(b) scope-separable**, hardening to (a) under single-arena ontology.
- **2 vs 3** (√2 vs √3): √π·ℤ ∩ √(2π/3)ℤ = {0} (ratio √6/2 irrational — note this one is *algebraic* irrationality, independent of π). **(b) scope-separable**: SU(2)₁ gate and phase-113 u(3) material are distinct arenas.
- **1 vs 5**: **(c) convention-dependent** (C-AD5). Reading A: a well-posed factor-2 refinement of the bridge lattice — but the refined lattice corresponds to doubling R (effective R² = 8π ∉ ℕ), destroying the exact C-BRIDGE pin R_Q = 1 and worsening the locality failure. Reading B: incommensurable (√(π/2) irrational, verified) — no refinement exists; the demand is unsatisfiable against demand 1. The conflict *dissolves into a refinement* under A and *hardens into incommensurability* under B; it is not adjudicable until C-AD5 is pinned [GAP-2].
- **2 vs 5**: q-lattices (1/√2)ℤ vs (1/2)ℤ: ratio 1/√2 irrational (verified), intersection {0} — incompatible under **either** C-AD5 reading (this pair's incommensurability is convention-independent, being a pure q-space statement). **(b) scope-separable** (level-1 vs level-2 arenas). Additionally moot in full form: demand 5's insufficiency finding means no R satisfies it entirely.
- **3 vs 5**: (1/√3)ℤ vs (1/2)ℤ: ratio √3/2 irrational, intersection {0}. **(b) scope-separable**.
- **6 vs all**: compatible with everything; ZM-1 joins every cluster for free.
- **Demand 1 internal tension** (mandated report): C-BRIDGE vs the extended-net construction itself. At R = √(2π): (i) winding sublattice Rℤ ⊂ Λ_R requires R ∈ (1/R)ℤ, i.e. R² ∈ ℤ — fails, so the winding structure is **undefined**, not merely non-local [BOX-1]; (ii) both locality grades fail. This is same-object and **not scope-separable**: it is the bridge's own extension. Whether it counts as a demand-vs-demand conflict depends on whether extended-net locality is held as binding on the bridge arena [GAP-1].

**Consequence [BOX-2]:** the three live pins form a genuine trilemma. There is no common refinement: a lattice containing two incommensurable lattices is dense, not a lattice. No hierarchy exists.
