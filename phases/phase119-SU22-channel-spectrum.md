## 2. K-SU22-2 ruling: is SU(2)₂ secretly abelian?

Three independent tests, each stated with its exact scope.

**(a) Single rank-1 lattice (the kill's literal wording).** Suppose h_{1/2} = 3/16 = q²/2, so q = √(3/8) = √6/4. A rank-1 lattice generated by q₀ = √6/4 assigns charge nq₀ the weight n²·(3/16). Computed spectrum mod 1: {0, 3/16, 11/16, 3/4} — **h = 1/2 never occurs** (3n² ≡ 8 mod 16 has no solution since n² mod 16 ∈ {0,1,4,9}). The three-sector structure {0, 3/16, 1/2} is not realizable on any lattice containing √6/4; verified by direct enumeration. Independently, c = 3/2 ≠ 1 blocks any single free boson realization [others']. This rules out *only* the single-boson rank-1 lattice reading.

**(b) Fusion-category obstruction (the decisive, realization-independent test).** In any abelian (pointed) fusion category — the categorical shadow of any lattice construction of any rank — every simple sector is invertible: d = 1 and a ⊗ b is a single sector [others']. SU(2)₂ has d_{1/2} = √2 ∉ ℤ and ½ ⊗ ½ = 0 ⊕ 1 (two channels). No abelian reduction of any rank, and no boson⊕boson decomposition, can reproduce the j = ½ sector. This is the strong sense in which the kill does not fire.

**(c) The Majorana caveat — evasion, not satisfaction.** c = 3/2 = 3 × 1/2: SU(2)₂ ≅ three free Majorana fermions ≅ so(3)₁ [others']. Also the Cartan coset decomposition SU(2)₂/U(1) = Ising holds: h_{1/2} = 1/8 + 1/16 (boson charge q = ½ contributing q²/2 = 1/8, plus Ising σ weight 1/16 — verified). So SU(2)₂ *contains* an abelian sub-structure, but the abelian part carries only 1/8 of the 3/16; the remainder (the σ-type sector, d = √2) is non-invertible and can never be a lattice sector. The Majorana realization is free and fermionic but is **not** "an abelian embedding on a rank-1 lattice": it *evades* the kill's wording rather than satisfying it. Honest residue **[GAP-1]**: SU(2)₂ is genuinely non-abelian but still free; the first non-abelian *and non-free* gate (e.g. SU(2)₃, or SU(3)) remains untested, and any freeness-exploiting shortcut hiding in later computations would not be caught by this gate.

**Ruling: K-SU22-2 DOES NOT FIRE.** SU(2)₂ is genuinely non-abelian in sense (b); sense (a) also fails abelian reduction; sense (c) is an explicit evasion, flagged.

## 3. K-SU22-1: the channel spectrum (main computation)

Two j = ½ defects on a carrier fuse in channels c ∈ {0, 1}. Transplantation candidate **[BOX-2]**: the pair in channel c carries the composite sector's rate on the carrier, D_c = 2h_cℓ:

| Channel c | h_c | N^c_{½½} | D_c | ΔD_c = D_c − 2D_{1/2} = D_c − 3ℓ/4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0 | 1 | 0 | **−3ℓ/4** (binding-like) |
| 1 | 1/2 | 1 | ℓ | **+ℓ/4** (anti-binding) |

**(i) Finite, distinct, well-defined.** Both rates are finite; 0 ≠ ℓ so the channels are dynamically distinguishable in rate; each intertwiner space Hom(c, ½⊗½) is one-dimensional (N^c_{½½} = 1) [others'], so there is no basis ambiguity within a channel — the rate assignment is unambiguous. Passes.

**(ii) Shape comparison with the abelian pair entry.** Abelian: D = (Q₁ + îQ₂)²ℓ₁/2π = D₁ + î²D₂ + 2îQ₁Q₂ℓ₁/2π — a base sum plus a cross term *linear* in the integer intersection datum î, symmetric under î → −î for equal charges (ΔD = ±2Q²ℓ/2π, mean zero over î = ±1). SU(2)₂: **what replaces î is the fusion-channel label c**, a two-valued discrete datum indexing the intertwiner space — not an integer of the geometric pairing but a representation-theoretic composite label. A cross-term analogue survives:

ΔD_c/2ℓ = h_c − h_{1/2} − h_{1/2} ∈ {−3/8, +1/8},

which is exactly the exponent of the braiding-monodromy eigenvalue θ_c/(θ_{1/2})² [others': ribbon identity]. So the channel-defect spectrum **is** the monodromy data of the modular category, in rate units — the non-abelian successor of the abelian cross term. Two structural deformations from the abelian case, both candidate signatures of genuine non-abelianity:
- **Asymmetry**: {−3ℓ/4, +ℓ/4} is not symmetric about 0 (abelian equal-charge case is ±).
- **Nonvanishing mean**: the unweighted channel average of ΔD is −ℓ/4 ≠ 0; the abelian î = ±1 average is 0. (Dimension-weighted average of h_c is 1/4 ≠ 2h_{1/2} = 3/8 — no weighting restores the abelian sum rule; checked.)

Channel 0 sits below the naive sum 2D_{1/2} = 3ℓ/4 by 3ℓ/4 (total screening: D = 0, the pair is rate-invisible — the non-abelian analogue of î giving Q₁ + îQ₂ = 0); channel 1 sits **above** by ℓ/4.

**(iii) GAP-N5 adjudication: is the channel label data?** The channel is the total sector charge of the pair — a superselection-type label: it is a label on the one-dimensional space Hom(c, ½⊗½), it is preserved under all operations local to the pair's complement, and it is in principle measurable on the pair (e.g. by a monodromy/interference protocol) without evolving anything in the record [others': anyon-model superselection]. Specifying c is *state specification*, on the same footing as specifying j for a single defect — admissible data under the successor thesis. **Honest residue [GAP-2]**: *which* channel a physically assembled pair occupies would, in any process account, be a dynamical fusion outcome (with natural weights d_c/(d_{1/2}d_{1/2}) = 1/2 each [others']); the record may carry c as data but cannot predict its value, and importing the probability weights would smuggle dynamics. The admissible record content is the *spectrum* {D_c}, not a selection among channels.

**Ruling: K-SU22-1 DOES NOT FIRE.** Rates are distinct, finite, well-defined; the label qualifies as data with residue [GAP-2] explicitly quarantined.
