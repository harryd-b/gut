## What qualifies as a number (the four conditions — all simultaneously)

1. **Derived, not fitted** — full chain auditable; every choice pinned BEFORE comparison.
2. **Confronted with measurement** via a matching rule registered BEFORE the number is computed.
3. **Surprising** — small P(match | framework false); generic/textbook/internal numbers never count (harmonic ratios, −1/24, spin-statistics).
4. **Knob-free** — the framework's free choices (genus, occupancy, lattice/arena, M-form, matching rule) must be pinned by internal argument first; a post-tuning match is a fit in a derivation's clothes.
