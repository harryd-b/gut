## T1 — Exact unitary orbits of states on the hyperfinite III₁ factor under outer automorphisms

**Question.** For faithful normal ω on the hyperfinite III₁ factor R∞ and φ = ω∘α with α outer, is φ in the *exact* unitary orbit {ω(u·u*)}?

**Verdict: NOT answered in the literature for a specific given state (such as the vacuum). The question sits precisely in the gap left open by the "pointwise inner automorphism" literature, which is the decisive adjacent art. Your completeness export is safe to frame as addressing an open question — but it must be positioned carefully against the results below.**

### What is proven

1. **Density (as you know).** Connes–Størmer, "Homogeneity of the state space of factors of type III₁", *J. Funct. Anal.* 28 (1978) 187–196 **[read: abstract via ScienceDirect/Springer search]**: for faithful normal φ, ψ on a III₁ factor with separable predual and ε > 0, there is a unitary u with ‖uφu* − ψ‖ < ε. This is norm-density only; III₁ is *characterized* by this topological transitivity.

2. **Exact orbit membership holds for modular automorphisms.** Definition (from Isono's paper, **[intro-read]** via ar5iv of arXiv:2309.05279): θ ∈ Aut(M) is *pointwise inner* if for every positive normal functional φ there is a unitary u with θ(φ) = uφu*. Haagerup–Størmer (1988–1990) **proved** that modular automorphisms σₜ^φ of type III₁ factors are pointwise inner — i.e., for α = σₜ^φ your question has answer YES, exactly, for every normal state. Source papers: U. Haagerup, E. Størmer, "Pointwise inner automorphisms of von Neumann algebras" (with appendix by C. Sutherland), *J. Funct. Anal.* 92 (1990) 177–201, and "Equivalence of normal states on von Neumann algebras and the flow of weights", *Adv. Math.* 83 (1990) 180–262. Caveat: I confirmed the pointwise-innerness of modular automorphisms from Isono's introduction **[intro-read]**, and confirmed the *Adv. Math.* paper's existence and bibliographic data **[read: ScienceDirect landing]**; I did not read the two 1990 Haagerup–Størmer papers themselves, and the exact division of results between them is inferred.

3. **The converse (Haagerup–Størmer conjecture, 1988) is now resolved for R∞.** Y. Isono, "Haagerup and Størmer's conjecture on pointwise inner automorphisms", *Compositio Math.* 160 (2024) 2480–2495, arXiv:2309.05279 **[read: abstract + intro summary]**: any type III₁ factor with trivial bicentralizer satisfies the conjecture — every pointwise inner automorphism is (inner)∘(modular). The proof uses Popa intertwining and Marrakchi's relative bicentralizer theory. Since Haagerup (1987) proved R∞ has trivial bicentralizer (Haagerup, "Connes' bicentralizer problem and uniqueness of the injective factor of type III₁", *Acta Math.* 158 (1987) — **[read: landing page]**), the theorem covers the hyperfinite III₁ factor. See also C. Houdayer, Y. Isono, "Pointwise inner automorphisms of almost periodic factors", *Selecta Math.* (2024), arXiv:2204.08344 **[read: abstract]** for the nonamenable almost-periodic class.

### What this implies for your question — and what remains open

- If your BMT defect automorphism α_η (Q ≠ 0) on A(I) ≅ R∞ is **not** of the form Ad(u)∘σₜ^φ, then by Isono's theorem α_η is *not pointwise inner*: there **exists** a normal positive functional ψ with ψ∘α_η outside the exact unitary orbit of ψ. (This is my inference from the cited theorems, not a statement I found; also note pointwise-innerness is defined over *all* positive functionals, so the witness ψ produced by failure is not guaranteed to be faithful, let alone equal to the vacuum.)
- **No result I found decides whether the vacuum specifically is such a witness.** The literature has no theorem of the form "for α outer and not inner∘modular, ω∘α ∉ {ω(u·u*)} for a *given* ω", nor a criterion for exact-orbit membership of a single state pair. Kawahigashi–Ogata–Størmer, "Normal states of type III factors", arXiv:1301.5737 (2013) **[read: abstract]** give only finite-dimensional-subalgebra exact matching (φᵢ∘Ad u = ω on a given finite-dimensional A ⊂ M), which is again an approximate-flavor result — the strongest "exactness" I found short of pointwise innerness.
- I found no literature on norm-closedness of the unitary orbit of a single faithful normal state on a III₁ factor. Searches for exact transitivity / non-closed orbits returned only the density results. I cannot rule out that this is buried in Haagerup–Størmer *Adv. Math.* 83 (1990) (which characterizes state equivalence via the flow of weights — trivial for III₁), which I could not read in full (ScienceDirect landing page only).

**Adjudication for T1: genuinely open** as posed (specific state, specific outer α). The honest framing for your export: Connes–Størmer gives density; Haagerup–Størmer gives exactness for the Inn·Mod class; Isono 2024 shows exactness-for-all-states *fails* outside Inn·Mod on R∞; whether the *vacuum* witnesses that failure for BMT automorphisms is not in the literature. If you can prove ω∘α_η is (or is not) in the exact orbit, that is a new result, and its natural home is exactly this pointwise-inner circle of ideas.

## T2 — Prior art for R1 (erasure rate D = Q²ℓ/2π)

**Verdict: the quadratic-in-charge-density relative entropy of BMT states on the U(1) current is established prior art (Longo 2019 and successors); the specific content of R1 — a profile-independent, purely topological rate Q²ℓ/2π indexed by geodesic length under discrete iteration of a hyperbolic element, with compensated single-valued profiles giving 0 — has no antecedent I could find. The continuous-modular-flow analogue of "linear growth of a cocycle/entropy quantity" is established (Ceyhan–Faulkner); the geodesic/discrete/charge-topological formulation appears novel.**

### Closest prior art, in order of proximity

1. **R. Longo, "Entropy of coherent excitations", *Lett. Math. Phys.* 109 (2019) 2587–2600, arXiv:1901.02366 [read: abstract; formula confirmed at second hand].** This is the foundational computation for exactly your objects: BMT automorphisms β_k of the U(1)-current net. The half-line relative entropy formula
  S_{A(I_t)}(ω∘β_k ‖ ω) = π ∫ₜ^∞ (x − t) k(x)² dx
  is quoted, with attribution to this Longo paper ([Lon20], their ref. 35), in Section 5.2 of Morinelli–Tanimoto–Wegener, "Modular operator for null plane algebras in free fields", *Comm. Math. Phys.* 395 (2022) 331–363, arXiv:2107.00039 **[intro/body-read via PMC]**. MTW explicitly do **not** iterate any transformation, and no geodesic length or hyperbolic element appears there. Honest caveat: I could not extract the formula from Longo's own text (the ar5iv fetch of 1901.02366 returned only the higher-dimensional free-scalar sections; the U(1) analysis is referenced to its Section 4 / a companion), so my attribution rests on MTW's quotation — verified **[read]** in MTW, second-hand for Longo.
  *Relevance:* this is quadratic in the charge density k (hence gives Q² scaling for concentrated profiles), interval/half-line local, for exactly BMT states. It is profile-dependent (an energy-type integral), not the topological Q²-only invariant of R1.

2. **F. Ciolli, R. Longo, G. Ruzzi, "The information in a wave", *Comm. Math. Phys.* 379 (2020) 979–1000, arXiv:1906.01707 [read: abstract].** Entropy of a classical wave; computes S(λ) under null translations λ of the Rindler wedge for localized-automorphism (sector) states and proves QNEC positivity d²S/dλ² ≥ 0. This is the closest prior art for *deforming the localization region along a one-parameter flow and tracking entropy growth* — but the flow is continuous null translation, not discrete iteration of a hyperbolic element, and no geodesic length appears.

3. **F. Ceyhan, T. Faulkner, "Recovering the QNEC from the ANEC", *Comm. Math. Phys.* 377 (2020) 999–1045, arXiv:1812.04683 [read: abstract + search-verified summary].** Connes-cocycle flow in half-sided modular inclusions; relative entropy derivative along null cuts = 2π × averaged null energy of optimal purifications. This is the established template for "cocycle flow ⇒ linear entropy/energy transfer relations." For a chiral charged state the ANE is quadratic in charge, so an expert could see a Q² × (flow parameter) rate as morally contained here — but only for the *continuous translation* flow, with no discrete-γ, no geodesic length ℓ, and no truncated-cocycle-norm formulation. Your D = Q²ℓ/2π under *iteration of a hyperbolic γ* (a dilation-type, not translation-type, one-parameter subgroup, discretized) is not in this paper.

4. **S. Hollands, "Relative entropy for coherent states in chiral CFT", *Lett. Math. Phys.* 110 (2020) 713–733, arXiv:1903.07508 [read: abstract + body summary via ar5iv].** Virasoro (stress-tensor) coherent states: S = c × Schwarzian action; Corollary 2 treats "solitonic states" from multivalued profiles. This is the nearest prior instance of the *single-valued vs. multivalued profile* dichotomy that underlies your "compensated single-valued profiles give 0" clause — but in the stress-tensor model, with no Q² charge formula extracted (I could not find one in the fetched content; the winding contribution is present but I did not verify its precise form).

5. **Also adjacent** (all **[read: abstract]** unless noted): H. Casini, S. Grillo, D. Pontello, "Relative entropy for coherent states from Araki formula", *Phys. Rev. D* 99 (2019) 125020, arXiv:1903.00109 (Rindler wedge, free scalar; no soliton/charge content found in fetched text — the soliton application is in Hollands' paper, not here, correcting my initial search impression); L. Panebianco, "A formula for the relative entropy in chiral CFT", *Lett. Math. Phys.* 110 (2020), arXiv:1911.10136, and "Loop groups and QNEC", *Comm. Math. Phys.* 387 (2021) **[snippet]** — solitonic representations of loop-group nets with explicit relative entropies, QNEC saturation, Bekenstein bounds; Casini–Huerta–Magán–Pontello, "Entanglement entropy and superselection sectors I", *JHEP* 02 (2020) 014, arXiv:1905.10487 — charged-sector relative entropies for global symmetries (I could **not** verify a q² log cross-ratio formula from the abstract; flagged unverified); R. Longo (with coauthors), "A Bekenstein-type bound in QFT", *Comm. Math. Phys.* (2025), arXiv:2409.14408 **[snippet]** — relative entropy of charged U(1)-current states on *bounded intervals*, the closest interval-local charged computation post-2019.

### Novelty adjudication for R1

- **Not novel:** relative entropy / cocycle-type quantities for BMT charged states being quadratic in the charge profile, computed interval- or half-line-locally (Longo 2019; MTW 2022; CLR 2020; Panebianco 2020–21).
- **Novel as far as I can determine:** (i) the *discrete-iteration* formulation (rate per application of γ); (ii) indexation by **geodesic length ℓ of a hyperbolic element**, i.e., the exact constant Q²ℓ/2π; (iii) the truncated-Connes-cocycle-norm² observable itself as the growth quantity; (iv) the sharp topological dichotomy "rate = Q²ℓ/2π vs. exactly 0 for compensated single-valued profiles" as an invariant of the defect class. A dedicated search for {relative entropy, conformal net} × {hyperbolic element, geodesic length, Fuchsian group} returned nothing relevant (only geodesic-flow ergodic theory and holography papers). The Fuchsian/double-coset refinement (R7) has, a fortiori, no antecedent I could find.
- **Framing risk:** an expert referee may say D = Q²ℓ/2π "is" 2h·(ℓ/2π) with h = Q²/2 the BMT conformal weight, hence "expected from" sector theory plus Bisognano–Wichmann, and morally parallel to Ceyhan–Faulkner's 2π⟨P⟩ rate. Cite Longo 2019 and Ceyhan–Faulkner prominently and claim novelty for the exact discrete/geodesic formulation and the erasure-dichotomy, not for "entropy ∝ Q²".
