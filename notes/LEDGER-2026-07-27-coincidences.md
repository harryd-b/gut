# THE COINCIDENCE LEDGER (opened 2026-07-27)

Companion to notes/PLAN-2026-07-27-numbers-mandate.md. Purpose: log every coincidence-shaped finding **including the ones that deflate**, so that survivors are not selection bias. A ledger of hits only is worthless; the graveyard is the calibration baseline.

## Admission tests (all three, before any entry is called evidence)

1. **Rigidity** — both sides fixed BEFORE contact; if either side has an adjustable knob, it is not a coincidence.
2. **Surprise under the null** — state explicitly what the deflationary hypothesis (this record is a re-labelled free chiral boson) predicts. If the null produces the same agreement, the entry is worth ZERO regardless of how striking it looks.
3. **Small search space** — integers and structures beat real numbers; unprompted agreements beat hunted ones. Symbol-matching (the same surd appearing twice) is NOT evidence; only a *constraint* linking the two sides counts.

## PART II — HUNTING LIST (cheap tests; run before any heavy program)

Ordered by (value × cheapness). Each states the test AND the null's prediction in advance.

**H1. The two arithmetics over ℚ(√2) — the strongest unexamined shape.** The geodesic side lives over ℚ(√2): all traces are p + q√2, the octagon uses s = √(2+2√2), the surface is arithmetic from a quaternion order over ℚ(√2). If independently variable → no evidence, close the entry. If some consistency requirement links them → this is the find, and it is where α lives. **Null's prediction: silent** — the free boson has no geodesic side at all, which is the property that makes a positive result surprising.

**H2. Cross-sector rate degeneracies.** D = Q²ℓ/2π draws Q from the charge lattice and ℓ from the length spectrum (cosh(ℓ/2) ∈ ℤ[√2]). **Cheap test:** small numeric scan over low charges × low lengths for *exact* coincidences D(Q₁,ℓ_i) = D(Q₂,ℓ_j) with (Q₁,ℓ_i) ≠ (Q₂,ℓ_j). Exact degeneracies would sharpen CAL-2's cross-sector interleaving from an ordinal claim into a hard one, and feed the SILVER window-count target directly. **Null's prediction:** generic incommensurability — accidental near-misses, no exact hits. An exact hit would need the two arithmetics to touch (see H1).

**H3. Is D ever required to be integral?** If any structural requirement forced D ∈ ℤ (or phases into 2πℤ) the condition Q²ℓ/2π ∈ ℤ becomes a Diophantine constraint welding the two arithmetics together. **Cheap test:** audit the refereed record for any single-valuedness/holonomy requirement on D itself (as opposed to on D_spin's phase). Current expectation: **nothing requires it** — D is registered as a rate, not a phase — in which case log the negative and close. Cheap, and it forecloses a whole family of false hopes.

**H4. The three "3"s.** The record contains three unconnected threes: the forced index-3 charge ratio (a lattice theorem), OCC-1's recurring three-orbit decomposition, and the empirical three generations. **Logged WITH its counterexamples, permanently:** OCC-1's ℓ₃ row has *four* orbits and ℓ₇ has *one*, so the three-orbit pattern is not a rule. **This is the most seductive and most dangerous entry in the ledger. **H5. An unexplained correlation between two independently-computed gradings is a genuine Tier-A shape. Null: silent (no surface). Caveat: quarantined behind the GAP-8 census anomaly.

**H6. Zero primitives at 2ℓ₁.** The level at exactly twice the systole contains no primitive geodesics at all (pure squared systoles). **Cheap test:** is this forced for arithmetic surfaces generally, or particular? If generic → deflate and close. Likely standard; log the negative either way.
