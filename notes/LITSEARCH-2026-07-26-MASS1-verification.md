# Literature Verification Report

## 1. Buniy–Kephart glueball/knot-mass model — VERIFIED

**Primary reference (abstract read directly on arXiv):** R. V. Buniy and T. W. Kephart, "A model of glueballs," Phys. Lett. B 576 (2003) 127–134; arXiv:hep-ph/0209339. Abstract (verbatim): "We model the observed glueball mass spectrum in terms of energies for tightly knotted and linked QCD flux tubes. The data is fit well with one parameter. We predict additional glueball masses."

**What is proportional to what:** The memory claim is accurate. In the follow-up paper (snippet from search results, consistent across multiple sources): for flux tubes of fixed uniform thickness and circular cross-section, "the length of the tight knot is proportional to the mass (or energy) of the knot" — i.e., glueball mass ∝ ropelength of the tight (ideal) knot/link, with a single fitted proportionality constant. The identified glueball states (f0 mesons) were matched against tight knot/link energies computed from ideal-knot ropelength data.

**Follow-up work exists:** Verified. R. V. Buniy and T. W. Kephart, "Glueballs and the universal energy spectrum of tight knots and links," Int. J. Mod. Phys. A 20 (2005) 1252; arXiv:hep-ph/0408027. Also "Universal energy spectrum of tight knots and links in physics," arXiv:hep-ph/0408025 (published in Physical and Numerical Models in Knot Theory, World Scientific "Series on Knots and Everything"). Kephart continued this line at least through 2012 (talk "The Tight Knot Spectrum in QCD," Edinburgh 2012), and a 2024 review-type arXiv paper "Tying knots in particle physics" (arXiv:2407.11731) covers this area (title/existence verified from search results only; I did not read its abstract in full).

## 2. Faddeev–Niemi knot solitons + Vakulenko–Kapitanskii bound — VERIFIED

**Faddeev–Niemi:** L. Faddeev and A. J. Niemi, "Stable knot-like structures in classical field theory," Nature 387, 58–61 (1997); arXiv:hep-th/9610193. The Nature listing was confirmed directly (nature.com/articles/387058a0); the paper demonstrates knot-like structures as stable finite-energy solitons (hopfions) in a 3+1d field theory (the Faddeev/Faddeev–Skyrme model). Title, authors, journal, volume, pages, year all match the memory claim exactly.

**Vakulenko–Kapitanskii bound:** Verified. A. F. Vakulenko and L. V. Kapitanskii, Sov. Phys. Dokl. 24, 433 (1979) (Dokl. Akad. Nauk SSSR original), on stability of solitons in the S² nonlinear sigma-model. The bound: E ≥ c·|Q|^{3/4} for Hopf charge Q, with the constant often quoted as c = (3/16)^{3/8} (in suitable units). Multiple independent secondary sources confirm this attribution and form (snippet-level confirmation from several papers citing it, including arXiv:hep-th/0106251 "Improving the Vakulenko-Kapitanskii type inequality for Hopfions" — note that specific arXiv entry was later withdrawn by its authors, but the citation trail is consistent). Importantly, rigorous work (F. Lin and Y. Yang, and Kundu–Rybakov lineage) established the matching **upper** bound E ≤ C|Q|^{3/4} in the Faddeev model — the sublinear growth is why knotted/clustered configurations beat separated unknots at large |Q| (Springer, Commun. Math. Phys. sources in search results). So "energy grows with topological complexity" is correct, but the growth is sublinear (power 3/4), not linear — a relevant nuance if comparing to a mass ∝ length proposal.

## 3. Ropelength / tight-knot energy literature — VERIFIED

**Anchor paper:** J. Cantarella, R. B. Kusner, J. M. Sullivan, "On the minimum ropelength of knots and links," Invent. Math. 150, 257–286 (2002); arXiv:math/0103224. Confirmed via Springer and arXiv listings. Content (from abstract/summary): ropelength = length/thickness; the paper proves existence and C^{1,1} regularity of ropelength minimizers in every knot/link type and gives improved lower bounds, some sharp. Ropelength minima are knot-type invariants — exactly the "ideal knot" literature claimed.

**Mapping to particle spectra beyond Buniy–Kephart:** Essentially NOT FOUND as an independent program. The Buniy–Kephart papers (and Kephart's later collaborations) appear to be the only sustained program mapping tight-knot ropelength to a particle (glueball) spectrum. Adjacent but distinct: (a) the Faddeev-knot energy growth-law literature (F. Lin & Y. Yang, "Universal growth law for knot energy of Faddeev type in general dimensions," Proc. R. Soc. A 464 (2008), per RSPA listing) treats knot energy as topology-dependent but for hopfions, not a particle mass spectrum fit; (b) "Ropelength and writhe quantization of 12-crossing knots" (Exp. Math. 34, 2025; arXiv:2305.17204) notes ropelength correlates with hyperbolic volume — mathematics, not particle physics.

## 4. Bilson-Thompson braid model, twist = charge — VERIFIED

**Reference:** S. O. Bilson-Thompson, "A topological model of composite preons," arXiv:hep-ph/0503213 (submitted 22 Mar 2005, revised 2006). I read the arXiv abstract directly: SM fermions and gauge bosons of the first generation are identified with elements of the braid group B₃ (framed ribbon braids, "3-belts"/helons). The arXiv abstract page shows no journal reference; it appears to have remained an arXiv preprint (the follow-up with Markopoulou and Smolin embedding it in loop quantum gravity was journal-published, Class. Quantum Grav. 2007 — not separately verified here).

**Twist ↔ charge assignment:** Verified at snippet level from multiple independent secondary sources (ResearchGate figure captions, arXiv:2501.03260 "Preons, Braid Topology, and Representations of Fundamental Particles," Int. J. Theor. Phys. 2025, and Bilson-Thompson's own 2007 Loops conference slides): each ribbon twist of ±2π represents electric charge ±e/3; helons H₊, H₀, H₋ carry Q = +1/3, 0, −1/3; total charge is the net twist (commutative, U(1)). Caveat: I could not parse the paper's PDF directly (encoding issue), so the exact in-paper sentence is not quoted; but the assignment is corroborated by at least three independent sources. The memory claim is accurate: **charge is twist; mass is not addressed** — no mass assignment appears in the abstract, and secondary literature consistently notes mass is not derived in the helon model.

## 5. Other proposals: mass = geodesic length / knot complexity — closest anchor is AdS₃/CFT₂

**AdS/CFT anchor (found, mainstream):** In AdS₃/CFT₂ the relation between heavy-operator conformal dimension and bulk geodesic length is standard: heavy operators create conical-defect (angle-deficit) geometries, and correlators/conformal blocks of operators with large Δ are dominated by geodesics, with the (regularized) geodesic length computing the block — e.g., "Holographic Entanglement Entropy from 2d CFT: Heavy States and Local Quenches" (Asplund–Bernamonti–Galli–Hartman, arXiv:1410.1392), the winding-geodesics/angle-deficit prescription (Ageev–Arefeva et al., arXiv ~1512.x, seen via ResearchGate), and "Monodromy and geometry of heavy-light Virasoro blocks" (arXiv:2607.09500). This is dimension↔geodesic-length (log of correlator), not literally a rest-mass spectrum, but it is the standard mainstream mass/Δ↔geodesic-length identification for heavy states.

**Direct "particle mass = closed-geodesic length on a hyperbolic surface" spectrum proposal:** NOT FOUND. A targeted search returned only pure mathematics on hyperbolic length spectra (Monk's thesis, arithmetic length-spectrum papers, simple-geodesic counting) with no particle-physics mass identification. **"Mass = word-length/knot complexity in a holographic setting":** NOT FOUND beyond the items above.

**Summary for the caller:** All four memory citations check out substantively (item 4's exact in-text sentence is snippet-corroborated rather than read from the PDF). Buniy–Kephart is genuine, direct prior art for "mass ∝ tight-knot length" (for glueballs, with quantized flux); Faddeev–Niemi/VK gives energy growing as |Q|^{3/4} (sublinear, a structural difference); Bilson-Thompson is prior art for twist=charge but explicitly not for mass. No published proposal identifying particle mass with closed-geodesic length on a hyperbolic surface was found; the AdS₃/CFT₂ heavy-operator/conical-defect geodesic relation is the closest mainstream anchor.
