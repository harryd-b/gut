# REFEREE REPORT — SU(2)₂ round (K-SU22-1, K-SU22-2)

## Verdict summary

The submission is substantially correct and both kill rulings are upheld, but not unscathed. I independently re-derived all modular data and every arithmetic claim (symbolic + numeric checks). Findings: (1) one outright error in the sector table — the spin θ₁ is −1, not i; had the submission used its own stated θ₁ in the ribbon check, its monodromy exponent +1/8 would have failed, so the error is isolated but real. (2) The "channel-defect spectrum IS monodromy data" identification is exponent-level only, and overclaims: monodromy is a phase fixing h_c−2h_{1/2} only mod 1; ΔD_c is a specific real lift. (3) The submission missed a genuine categorical sum rule: the dimension-weighted monodromy phases sum exactly to zero (because S_{½½}=0 in SU(2)₂), i.e. d₀e^{−3πi/4}+d₁e^{+πi/4}=0 — an abelian-like cancellation at phase level. The claimed non-abelian signature survives because it lives at rate (real) level, where the probability-weighted mean −ℓ/4 ≠ 0; but "no weighting restores the sum rule" is false as universally quantified. (4) The unit-fork "half-lattice / factor-2" claim is correct only under one reading of R and catastrophically wrong under the other; it must be convention-pinned. (5) K-SU22-2's survival is stronger than the submission argues (all-rank central-charge kill), and the "EVADES" self-labeling is too timid — the kill does not fire in wording or spirit; GAP-1 is the honest residue. (6) The proposed successor gate "SU(3)" is unlevel-tagged; SU(3)₁ is pointed abelian and would be a vacuous gate.

## Per-claim analysis

**Sector data.** h_j = j(j+1)/(k+2): (0, 3/16, 1/2) ✓. c = 3k/(k+2) = 3/2 ✓. d_j = sin((2j+1)π/4)/sin(π/4) = (1, √2, 1) ✓; 𝒟 = √(1+2+1) = 2 ✓. Fusion rules are the standard truncated Clebsch-Gordan at k=2 ✓ [others': standard]. Spins θ_j = e^{2πih_j}: (1, e^{3πi/8}, **−1**). The submission's θ₁ = i is wrong (i would require h=1/4). Verified numerically: θ₁ = e^{2πi·(1/2)} = −1. Candidate rates D_j = 2h_jℓ = (0, 3ℓ/8, ℓ) ✓ (conditional on BOX-1, properly flagged).

**Mod-16 argument.** Enumerated: n² mod 16 ∈ {0,1,4,9}; 3n² mod 16 ∈ {0,3,11,12}; 8 never occurs ✓. Rank-1 spectrum with q = √(3/8): h_n = 3n²/16, mod 1 = {0, 3/16, 11/16, 3/4} ✓ (verified by direct enumeration); h = 1/2 unreachable ✓.

**Pointed-category claim.** As imported: even-lattice VOAs of any rank yield pointed modular categories (simples = L*/L, all invertible, d=1, single-channel fusion) — correct [others': standard]. Since d_{1/2} = √2 and ½⊗½ has two channels, no abelian record of any rank can host the sector, whether by equivalence or embedding. I add a stronger, simpler all-rank argument the submission only deployed at rank 1: bosonic lattice CFTs have c = rank ∈ ℤ; c = 3/2 ∉ ℤ excludes every bosonic lattice realization at a stroke. Orbifolds/cosets of lattice theories are not lattice theories and don't rescue the kill as registered.

**Coset arithmetic.** SU(2)₂/U(1) = Ising, c: 3/2 − 1 = 1/2 ✓; h_{1/2} = 1/8 + 1/16 = 3/16 ✓, with boson part q = 1/2 → q²/2 = 1/8 and Ising σ = 1/16 ✓. Cartan weights m²/8 consistent with m²/(4k) at k=2 ✓.

**K-SU22-2, Direction 1 (is survival right?).** Yes, and robustly. Rank-2 and higher bosonic lattices: killed by c ∉ ℤ and by pointedness. Fermionic route: three Majoranas (so(3)₁ ≅ SU(2)₂) is a spin theory requiring a spin structure; an odd rank-1 lattice gives one Dirac = two Majoranas, not three; no legitimate sense of "lattice" makes three Majoranas "an abelian embedding on a rank-1 lattice." The registered kill cannot be made to fire.

**K-SU22-2, Direction 2 (is the evasion ruling honest?).** The submission's "EVADES the kill's wording" is, if anything, over-confessional. The kill's target is "genuinely non-abelian." The fusion category is genuinely non-abelian by any standard criterion (non-invertible simple, d = √2, two-channel fusion, S_{½½} = 0) — the Ising/Majorana anyons are the canonical non-abelian anyons, and their free-fermion realizability does not make them abelian. Ruling: DOES NOT FIRE on wording **and** on spirit. The honest residue — non-abelian ≠ non-free — is a different, unregistered kill, and GAP-1 logs it correctly. Successor gate: SU(2)₃ (Fibonacci sector, non-free, quantum-universal) is the right gate; bare "SU(3)" is defective — SU(3)₁ is pointed (ℤ₃, all d=1) and would pass trivially in the wrong direction; must be SU(3)_k≥2, and the submission's own level-tagging rule (unit-fork obs. 5) condemns its own untagged proposal.

**K-SU22-1 (i) ribbon identification.** Verified: θ₀/θ_{1/2}² = e^{−3πi/4} = e^{2πi(−3/8)}; θ₁/θ_{1/2}² = e^{+πi/4} = e^{2πi(1/8)} (numerically confirmed: phase args/2π = −0.375, +0.125). Exponents match ΔD_c/2ℓ = h_c − 2h_{1/2} ∈ {−3/8, +1/8} ✓. Note this check only passes with the correct θ₁ = −1; the submission's tabulated θ₁ = i gives −1/8, contradicting its own claim — evidence the table entry is a transcription error, not a propagated one. However, "the channel-defect spectrum IS the monodromy data in rate units" is an overclaim: the monodromy eigenvalue determines the exponent only mod 1; ΔD_c = 2ℓ(h_c−2h_{1/2}) is a specific real lift (−3/8, not +5/8). The rate spectrum strictly refines the monodromy data. Substantive match at exponent level; rebranding at the "IS" level.

**K-SU22-1 (ii) abelian comparison and sum rules.** Abelian equal-charge pair: D = Q²(1+î)²ℓ/2π; ΔD relative to 2 singles = [(1+î)²−2]Q²ℓ/2π = ±2Q²ℓ/2π for î = ±1 — symmetric, mean 0 ✓. Non-abelian ΔD ∈ {−3ℓ/4, +ℓ/4}: asymmetric, unweighted mean −ℓ/4 ✓; dimension-weighted mean of h_c = (0+1/2)/2 = 1/4 ≠ 3/8 ✓; canonical fusion-probability weights d_c/d_{1/2}² = (1/2, 1/2) give mean ΔD = −ℓ/4 ≠ 0 ✓. **But**: the known categorical identity Σ_c N^c d_c θ_c/(θ_aθ_b) = 𝒟·S_{ab} evaluates here to zero, since S_{½½} = (1/√2)sin(π) = 0 (verified: my numeric sum d₀e^{−3πi/4} + d₁e^{+πi/4} = 0 exactly). So an abelian-like cancellation **is** restored at the phase level — the submission's "no weighting restores the sum rule" is wrong as stated. Two saving graces: (a) the cancellation is of phases e^{2πiΔ}, not of the real rates ΔD_c, and no nonnegative canonical weighting zeroes the real spectrum; (b) S_{½½} = 0 is a special feature of ½ at k=2, worth recording as structure, not as a defeat of the signature. The signature claim survives, narrowed: it is a rate-level (lift-level) asymmetry, coexisting with an exact phase-level sum rule. Also, one soft point: the abelian mean averages over geometric data î at fixed charges, the non-abelian mean over fusion channels at fixed geometry — the ensembles differ, so "asymmetry about 0" is a suggestive, not apples-to-apples, contrast. Also note the weighting w = (1/4, 3/4) does restore the mean to 3/8; it is non-canonical, but the claim should read "no canonical weighting."

**K-SU22-1 (iii) GAP-N5.** Rates (0, 3ℓ/8, ℓ): finite ✓; channel rates {0, ℓ} distinct ✓; N^c_{½½} = 1 kills basis ambiguity ✓; D₀ = 0 is well-defined (vacuum channel rate-invisible is a prediction, not a pathology). The superselection argument for the channel label as data is sound: the label on Hom(c, ½⊗½) is preserved under complement-local operations. But the "measurable by monodromy protocol" justification smuggles an operation (braiding/transport is evolution) into the admissibility argument; admissibility should rest on the label being holonomy-type data, with the protocol demoted to an in-principle external witness. Flag, not kill. The quarantine of channel selection (weights d_c/d_{1/2}²) as excluded dynamics is correct and is the load-bearing move; GAP-2 properly logged. Ruling: DOES NOT FIRE, explicitly conditional on BOX-1/BOX-2 candidate status, which the submission itself concedes (GAP-N1).

**Unit-fork audit.** Arithmetic: √(π/2) = √(2π)/2 = 1.2533… exactly ✓ (verified). But the "HALF-lattice points / factor-2 refinement" claim is convention-dependent. Reading A (R = charge-lattice unit, lattice Q ∈ √(2π)ℤ, hence refereed q ∈ ℤ and D = n²ℓ — "record-integer" rates): Cartan charges at Q-spacing √(2π)/2 are exactly half-lattice points; factor-2 refinement correct. Reading B ("record-integer" = record charges Q ∈ ℤ, q ∈ (1/√(2π))ℤ ≈ 0.3989ℤ): Cartan needs q ∈ (1/2)ℤ, and 0.5/0.3989 = √(2π)/2 is irrational — the lattices are **incommensurable**; no finite refinement embeds one in the other, and the claim fails totally. The submission's own numbers (spacing √(π/2) called "half-lattice") are self-consistent only under Reading A. Until GAP-N7 pins the convention, the factor-2 claim is unproven; under Reading B it is false. This must be recorded. Conformal-embedding observation: su(2)₂ ⊗ Ising ⊂ su(2)₁ ⊕ su(2)₁ with c: 3/2 + 1/2 = 1 + 1 ✓ is the standard GKO statement [others'], and su(2)₁ = self-dual boson links to R = √2 plausibly. No-√3 claim: correct — the k=2 simple current (j=1, θ = −1, ℤ₂ fusion) is fermionic; no ℤ₃ structure anywhere in the fusion ring ✓. Level-moving spinor sub-charge: k=1 gives h = 1/4 → q = 1/√2; k=2 gives h = 1/8 → q = 1/2 ✓ (verified); level-tagging mandate justified.

## Corrections ledger

- **C-AD1** (error): θ₁ = −1, not i (h₁ = 1/2 → e^{2πi/2}). Table must be fixed; the submission's own ribbon check contradicts its own tabulated spin.
- **C-AD2** (overclaim): "spectrum IS monodromy data in rate units" → downgrade to "the channel-defect exponents reduce mod 1 to the monodromy exponents"; ΔD_c is a strict lift, monodromy fixes it only mod 2ℓ·1.

"No weighting restores the sum rule" is false as stated; amend to "no canonical nonnegative weighting restores the real (rate-level) sum rule; the phase-level sum rule holds and S_{½½}=0 should be recorded."
- **C-AD4** (imprecision): the abelian-mean vs channel-mean contrast averages over different ensembles (geometric datum î vs fusion channel c); the asymmetry signature should be stated as a within-record spectral property, not an ensemble comparison.
- **C-AD5** (convention gap): the factor-2 lattice-refinement claim is valid only under the "lattice = Rℤ = √(2π)ℤ" reading; under "record charges Q ∈ ℤ" the Cartan and record lattices are incommensurable (ratio √(2π)/2 irrational) and no refinement exists. Pin the GAP-N7 convention before this observation is used.
- **C-AD6** (defective gate): "SU(3)" successor gate must be level-tagged; SU(3)₁ is pointed abelian and vacuous. Use SU(2)₃ (Fibonacci) or SU(3)_k≥2.
- **C-AD7** (mislabel): "EVADES the kill's wording" understates the result — the free-fermion realization does not make the category abelian; K-SU22-2 is genuinely survived in wording and spirit. GAP-1 (non-abelian ≠ non-free) stands as a new gate, not as an evasion confession.
- **C-AD8** (flag): grounding the channel label's data-status in a "monodromy protocol" invokes an operation the record cannot express; rest admissibility on superselection/holonomy structure alone.
