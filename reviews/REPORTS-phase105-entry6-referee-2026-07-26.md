Absent that, "mirroring the physical defect flips D₁₂" is true by the only available definition, but the stronger claim "the winding-implementer mirror anomaly is resolved" would overreach: it is reclassified as ill-posed, not answered. §2.4(α) is a plausible and correct diagnosis of *why* the winding computation degenerates (the non-periodic part defeats the kill-the-constant step); (β) is fair.
