## B. Mathematics of the imports — two real hits, several passes

**Passes.** Wood 1971 (|e| ≤ 2g−2 for topological flat/foliated circle bundles): correct. Goldman/Matsumoto–Ghys maximal-representation rigidity: correct. Reeb orbits are positively transverse to ξ (α(R) = 1 > 0): trivially correct, and K_γ is a Reeb orbit, not an S¹-fiber (the fibers are Legendrian, per the phase-107 amendment — no conflict). H₁(T¹Σ_g) = ℤ^{2g} ⊕ ℤ/(2g−2): correct (Gysin; fiber class of order 2g−2); [K_γ] over nonseparating γ has infinite order, over separating γ at most torsion: correct. Eliashberg tight Bennequin sl ≤ 2g−1 = −χ(Σ_Seifert), null-homology hypothesis, one-sidedness under stabilization (sl drops without bound): correctly stated; the notation "g_s" is hazardous (usually slice genus) but the submission's own equation to −χ(Σ_Seifert) fixes the meaning as Seifert genus, which is the genus Eliashberg's bound actually uses. Gay (positive Giroux torsion obstructs strong filling) and Ghiggini (weak-filling counterpoint): as registered in C6. Hurder–Katok Publ. IHÉS 72 / Mitsumatsu formula with constant-curvature rigidity: consistent with phases 45/46 as quoted.

**C-X1 (MAJOR — the rational-sl import fails on the main domain).** Baker–Etnyre's rational self-linking exists only for **rationally null-homologous** knots ([K] torsion in H₁, i.e. 0 in H₁(·;ℚ)). Over nonseparating γ, [K_γ] has **infinite order** — it is not rationally null-homologous, no rational Seifert surface exists, and sl_ℚ(K_γ) is **undefined**. Since KNOT-Q v2 realizes charge precisely over essential simple geodesics — generically nonseparating — the C-SL bound, even granted the postulate, applies only to the separating-carrier subfamily. The verdict block's "rational-sl version for non-null-homologous carriers" and the header's "(rational version for most carriers)" are **false**: for most carriers there is no self-linking number of any kind, so C-SL does not even typecheck there without a further, nonexistent-in-the-literature extension. GAP-B's body text half-knows this (it attaches the rational bound to the separating case) but the advertised conclusion contradicts its own gap. Verdict lines must be rewritten: *bound established (modulo C-SL) only for separating carriers; nonseparating carriers currently unboundable by any Bennequin technology.*

**C-X2 (MAJOR — missed parity consistency check; the "consistent with every refereed n-dependent formula" claim is refuted as stated).** Transverse stabilization changes sl by −2, so the sl-values realized by transverse representatives of a fixed knot type lie in a **single parity class** (in the null-homologous case, sl ≡ −χ(Σ) = 2g−1 mod 2, i.e. **sl is odd** — textbook in S³, and χ(Σ) = 1−2g is odd for every Seifert surface). Under C-SL as drafted ("n equals the self-linking number of a transverse representative"), the framing sector is confined to one parity — in the flagship case **odd n only**. But the refereed record realizes **all** n ∈ ℤ on the boundary side, and in particular the **n = 0 rigid-transport sector is the refereed, numerically verified default** (phase105-Dspin: "n = 0 for rigid transport"; referee confirmed rigid-rotation framing to 10⁻⁹). C-SL therefore either declares half the refereed sectors — including the constructed default — physically unrealizable, or is inconsistent. This is strictly worse than the J-mirror tension the submission flags (J maps n ↦ −n, parity-preserving, so J is actually fine; the parity clash is the real one, and it was missed). It must enter the gap list as a named gap (suggest GAP-F) before any registration decision; it is also an ironic structural echo of the refereed **e-odd** theorem on the charge side, which a defender might try to spin as support — that spin, if attempted, must be argued, not assumed.

**C-X4 (minor).** "Milnor 1958 (linear case, bound 2g−2 up to convention)": Milnor's linear (GL⁺(2,ℝ)) bound is |e| ≤ g−1; 2g−2 is the PSL(2,ℝ)/Homeo⁺(S¹) bound (derivable from Milnor for PSL₂, proved topologically by Wood). "Up to convention" papers over a genuine group-dependence, not a sign convention. No downstream impact (the carrier's e = ±(2g−2) sits at the Wood/PSL bound, which is the relevant one).

## C. Verdict logic

**MW closure: CONFIRMED**, and it is airtight where it matters. Any MW-type inequality — including a hypothetical one applied after insertion — bounds an Euler number: of the fixed bundle (unchanged by definition), or of ξ evaluated on surfaces (Eliashberg's |⟨e(ξ),[Σ]⟩| ≤ 2g−2 for tight ξ — which factors through e(ξ), unchanged by refereed Box 2), or of a foliated structure (none exists canonically post-insertion, refereed C-M3). Every MW-shaped functional in sight factors through homotopy-level data, which C-M1 makes n_tor-independent; and n_fr has no bulk referent at all. **C-X5 (phrasing only):** "no postulate can reopen this route" overreaches slightly — a postulate can always import a new MW-capped object (exactly as C-SL imports a Bennequin-capped one); the defensible claim is that no identification of n with any object in the record's inventory gives MW anything to grip. Substance stands.

**GV closure: CONFIRMED.** Real-valued, attached to a foliation that does not survive insertion (C-M3), blind to the boundary net; the only conceivable functional route is literally GV-T, shelved as unposed (FR-6). I probed for an unconsidered route (contact/bi-contact GV analogues à la Mitsumatsu–Hozoori): any such route is new mathematics of exactly GV-T's shape, not an omission from the record. "Honestly empty" is fair.

**Bennequin route: PARTIAL.** The verdict *class* — NEEDS-POSTULATE, unique live route, previous deployment a refereed category error, re-aim at the carrier Reeb orbit legitimate (transverse, tight, tightness retained) — is CONFIRMED, and the re-aim is genuinely clever: it survives the phase-107 category-error ruling because it caps a framing datum via sl rather than licensing twist admissibility. But the advertised deliverable is overstated twice: the bound's domain excludes most carriers (C-X1), and the consistency sweep is incomplete and, as stated ("consistent with every refereed n-dependent formula"), wrong (C-X2). Gaps A–E are honestly flagged as far as they go; the list is short by one major entry (parity) and GAP-B is mislabeled (not "import unchecked" but "imported tool provably inapplicable on the main domain"). E3/M-FRAME/bridge-tower/J-mirror checks are otherwise correctly reasoned — the n = 2 M-FRAME rows are indeed scheme-decider counterfactuals, and the tower/e-odd results do concern the torsion integer, per my §A.5 verification.

**Watch item: PARTIAL — the dichotomy is not forced. C-X3 (moderate):** "Either the protection is tightness (no cap) or fillability (everything dead); no intermediate exists on that axis" ignores the reading the record itself registers between them: **weak fillability** (C6 distinguishes it explicitly; weakly fillable ⟹ tight ⟹ strictly intermediate as a protection level; note also the carrier is *exactly* fillable, so the strong reading is available, making the axis live). Ghiggini-type examples show torsion does not obstruct weak filling in general, but whether arbitrary torsion insertion on T¹Σ_g preserves weak fillability is nowhere in the record — an intermediate finite cap on this axis is not excluded by anything refereed. The watch item should be logged as a trichotomy with the weak-fillability branch OPEN, not a forced binary.
