**Referee report: SPAN-1 torsion-insertion derivation (C1–C8)**

**Summary.** The differential-geometric core of this derivation is sound, and I verified the load-bearing computations myself (sl₂ matrix algebra for transversality; layer volume and Reeb field by symbolic computation). C1, C2, C3, and the anchor argument C5(iii) are correct — C2 is in fact correct for a stronger, uniform reason the authors missed. The genuinely weak links are exactly where the draft flags them, with two exceptions: the C4(iv) "torsion pairing" argument is a partial strawman that oversells Route 2's conclusion, and the C6 "underdetermination" framing understates a real asymmetry — the e = 0 horn is (conditionally) a theorem while the e = ±1 horn is a bare postulate, so presenting them as symmetric "determining choices" is generous to the physics. GAP-3 (loss of the Anosov structure post-insertion) is honestly flagged but strategically undersold: it undermines the very leaf space on which the charge is supposed to be read.

**1. C1 — Local geometry. Verdict: CORRECT.**
Computed directly: on T_γ parametrize by (s, θ); dπ(∂_s) = c′(s), dπ(∂_θ) = 0, so α_can(∂_s) = ⟨u(s,θ), c′(s)⟩ = cos θ and α_can(∂_θ) = 0, giving α_can|_{T_γ} = cos θ ds as claimed. The restricted form vanishes identically on T T_γ exactly at cos θ = 0, and since both T T_γ and ξ are 2-planes, T T_γ = ξ precisely on the circles θ = ±π/2. Against the standard definition (pre-Lagrangian ⟺ α restricts to a closed nowhere-zero form, equivalently nonsingular linear characteristic foliation, equivalently Lagrangian lift to the symplectization): cos θ ds is neither closed (d(cos θ ds) = sin θ ds∧dθ ≠ 0) nor nowhere-zero (dies on the tangency circles), and the characteristic foliation has two full circles of singularities — a maximally non-generic, non-convex position. So "not pre-Lagrangian/convex as it sits" is right, and the GAP-1 caveat (Colin-type normal-form positioning before insertion) is exactly the right caveat, since torsion insertion requires a pre-Lagrangian or standard-neighborhood model.

**2. C2 — Transversality. Verdict: CORRECT (and provable more strongly).**
I ran the sl₂ computation. Frame: X = ½diag(1,−1) (spray), V = ½[[0,1],[−1,0]] (vertical), Θ = ½[[0,1],[1,0]] (horizontal-normal), U± strictly upper/lower triangular. Verified: [X,U⁺] = U⁺, [X,U⁻] = −U⁻; under the geodesic flow (right multiplication by exp(tX)) the left-invariant field U⁺ pushes forward by Ad(exp(−tX))U⁺ = e^{−t}U⁺, so U⁺ is the stable horocycle direction. Verified U± = Θ ± V: the claim "stable Jacobi direction is a nontrivial vertical/horizontal-normal mix" is *literally exact*. At θ = 0 the torus tangent plane is span(X, V) (the horizontal lift of c′ at u = c′ is the spray, since c is a geodesic); det[X, V, U⁺] = −1 ≠ 0, so U⁺ ∉ T T_γ — the claimed check at the closed orbits is verified. The tangency-circle argument (T T_γ = ξ, Reeb ∈ TW_weak-stable, Reeb ∉ ξ) is also correct.

The authors missed a one-line uniform proof: TW = span(X, U⁺) is constant in the left-invariant frame (weak-stable leaves are orbits of the Borel subgroup AN), and det[X, U⁺, V] = 1 ≠ 0, so **V ∉ TW anywhere**, while V ∈ T T_γ everywhere. Two 2-planes in a 3-manifold are transverse iff unequal; hence transversality holds at every point of T_γ with no case analysis. (I also checked the general-θ span: det[X, U⁺, cos θ X − sin θ Θ] = sin θ, whose zeros at θ = 0, π are covered by the V-argument.) Claim stands, exception-free.

**3. C3 — Layer data. Verdict: CORRECT.**
Symbolically verified: α_n ∧ dα_n = 2πn dt∧dx∧dy (positive contact for n > 0); volume ∫ = 2πn·ℓ·2π = 4π²nℓ; α_n(∂_t) = 0 so ∂_t ∈ ξ_n(t) for every t; plane trace winds n full turns as t: 0→1. The 2πn convention matches the standard integer Giroux-torsion domain (as in Ghiggini–Honda–Van Horn-Morris).

**4. C4 — Route 2 (Euler class). Verdict: CORRECT WITH REPAIR.**
(i) Verified: ∂_t ∈ ker α_n identically and is nowhere zero, so ξ_n is trivialized over the layer by ∂_t; if ∂_t matches the chosen boundary reference section (it does, up to the standard collar identification in which ∂_t is the co-orientation of T_γ, which lies in ξ_can on the tangency-free collar after normal-form positioning — this quietly *uses* GAP-1), the relative Euler class in H²(T²×I, ∂) vanishes and e(ξ_new) = e(ξ_can) in H²(X;ℤ). (ii) H²(T²×I, ∂; ℤ) ≅ H₁(T²×I) ≅ ℤ² by Lefschetz duality — correct. (iii) The T³ import (Giroux/Kanda: all ξ_n homotopic as plane fields) and GHV (torsion insertion preserves the homotopy class of ξ) are the right citations in spirit, but the rel-boundary secondary obstruction (d³-type, valued in a quotient of H³(layer, ∂)) is not the same computation as the closed-T³ one; GAP-2 is correctly flagged and genuinely open at the stated level of rigor. **Repair needed at (iv):** the argument "any H₁-pairing through the fiber class lands in ℤ/(2g−2), incompatible with Q ∈ ℤ" is internally coherent (the fiber class is the torsion generator of order 2g−2, so any ℤ-valued homomorphism kills it), but it only excludes pairings through the *fiber* class. It does not exclude pairings through [c_γ] ∈ ℤ^{2g}, which is non-torsion — and that is where the anchored charge actually sits. As written, (iv) refutes a strawman; the honest Route-2 conclusion is carried entirely by (i)–(iii): *torsion is invisible to plane-field homotopy (modulo GAP-2), so any charge that factors through homotopy is independent of n* — which for the n-dependence question is enough, but the blanket "e cannot be a homotopy pairing" should be struck or restricted.

**5. C5 — Route 1. Verdicts: (i) CORRECT; (ii) CORRECT; (iii) CORRECT; (iv) UNPROVEN.**
(i) Verified symbolically: R_n = cos(2πnt)∂_x − sin(2πnt)∂_y satisfies α_n(R_n) = 1 and i_{R_n}dα_n = 0 exactly (the "∝" is an equality). Level tori are invariant with linear flow of t-rotating slope; a flow with a 1-parameter family of invariant tori is not Anosov. GAP-3 is real and, I would argue, **undersold**: the boundary circle S¹ = ∂ℍ is the leaf space of the *pre-insertion* weak-stable foliation. After insertion there is no canonical foliation, hence no canonical leaf space, hence the object on which the defect line and its anchors live is a structure carried over by fiat from the old flow. Route 1's entire reading (winding → wall → leaf-space defect) is a statement about the old foliation decorated by new contact data; this should be stated as such, not merely footnoted.
(ii) The relative winding of ξ along a transverse arc crossing the layer is n, independent of the arc and trivialization choices (both boundary planes are ξ_can-matched); this is the standard well-definedness of torsion as a germ invariant. Fine.
(iii) **Anchor argument verified.** γ simple ⟹ its lifts are pairwise disjoint complete geodesics ⟹ the preimage of T_γ in T¹ℍ is a disjoint Γ-orbit of cylinders ℝ×S¹, one per lift; the cylinder over axis(ξ₋,ξ₊) has ideal trace exactly {ξ±}. For ξ ∉ {ξ±}: for each p on the axis A there is exactly one unit vector at p pointing at ξ (uniqueness of the geodesic ray from p to ξ in ℍ), so W_ξ ∩ (cylinder) = {u_p : p ∈ A} ≅ ℝ — a single properly embedded arc, and the intersection is transverse by the uniform argument in §2 (V ∈ T(cylinder) \ TW). The degeneration locus on the leaf space is exactly {ξ±} (W_{ξ±} meets the cylinder in the closed-orbit lines θ = 0, π). So the wall reads on S¹ as a defect with endpoints fix(γ): **anchors forced**, consistent with the refereed locality theorem — modulo the GAP-3 caveat that "the leaf space" means the pre-insertion one.
(iv) The homomorphism k ↦ e·k from winding to charge is *postulated*, and |e| = 1 rests on the unproven normalization axiom (N). GAP-4 correctly flagged; nothing here is derived from the net axioms. UNPROVEN, as the draft admits.

**6. C6 — Kill evaluation. Verdict: CORRECT WITH REPAIR (honesty acceptable, framing asymmetric).**
No hidden fitting detected: both horns are stated with their assumptions, and the pre-kill argument against homotopy-factoring ("it annihilates the refereed bulk datum") is correctly quarantined as definitional [GAP-6] — it is a methodological preference, not a theorem, since a dictionary in which the boundary net sees only homotopy data is not internally contradictory, merely trivializing. **Repair:** the two horns are not epistemically symmetric and the "underdetermination with a determining choice" language obscures this. Horn 1 (e = 0) follows from Route 2, i.e. from mathematics, conditional only on GAP-2. Horn 2 (e = ±1) follows from two stacked postulates (GAP-4 homomorphism + axiom (N)) with no derivation from the net. Underdetermination between a conditional theorem and a bare postulate should be labeled as such. The contractible-projection clause of the KNOT-Q kill is safe unconditionally: insertion is only defined along π⁻¹(c_γ) for essential simple c_γ, every nontrivial element of a cocompact Fuchsian group is hyperbolic, so no contractible-projection class can ever acquire visible charge in this construction. Confirmed.

**7. C7 — Simplicity. Verdict: CORRECT (as far as checkable).**
Non-simple c ⟹ π⁻¹(c) is only immersed (self-intersecting along whole fibers over double points of c, a non-generic clean intersection). Giroux torsion insertion requires an embedded T²×I neighborhood; I know of no torsion-insertion operation along immersed tori in the literature (Colin, Honda–Kazez–Matić, GHV all use embedded incompressible tori), and higher-genus analogues are not known to preserve tightness. GAP-7 is the right flag, and prediction (s) is genuinely nonvacuous.

**8. C8 — Consistency. Verdict: CORRECT, with one red flag properly disclosed.**
Anchors, additivity (given the postulated homomorphism), and the γ→γ⁻¹ sign flip (fix(γ⁻¹) swaps ξ±, orientation of c and hence winding sign reverses) are consistent. D = e²n²ℓ/2π is honest arithmetic from Q = en in the refereed D = Q²ℓ/2π. The disclosed mismatch — layer contact volume linear in n versus D quadratic in n — is real, correctly not papered over, and is a substantive strike against any volume-based derivation of D from the insertion.
